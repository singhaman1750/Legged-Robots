### 2.2 Legged Robot Control
Model-based control (MPC, WBC) and learning-based control (RL) for quadrupeds and humanoids, including papers on emergent/interesting behaviors.
→ [topics/legged-robot-control.md](topics/legged-robot-control.md)

### 2.3 Legged Robot Mechanical Design & Co-Design
Design principles, quadruped and humanoid design papers, design/co-design optimization, and design patents.
